Forgot your password?

VitalGate runs on a hospital network with no outgoing email, so passwords are reset by an administrator on the VitalGate host. Nothing is sent over the internet.

  1. Contact your VitalGate administrator (the person who runs the console on the security workstation) and tell them your username.
  2. The administrator sets a temporary password from the VitalGate folder:
    python server.py --add-user <your username>
  3. Sign in and change it under Settings → Account → Change password. Other sessions are signed out automatically.
